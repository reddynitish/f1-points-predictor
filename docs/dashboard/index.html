THE ENGINEERING BEHIND THE FORECAST

A simple model. A careful process.

01 / DATA

Inputs have a cutoff.

History comes from earlier events. Whole-team results are aggregated before shifting, so a teammate’s target-race result cannot enter the features.

02 / EVALUATION

Whole races stay together.

Development moves forward by season. Configurations were frozen before holdouts. The spent 2025 and 2026 tests cannot be reused to prove another model.

03 / DECISION

Keep the baseline when it wins.

Richer inputs failed to demonstrate additional value. Negative findings, coverage gaps and uncertainty are part of the result.
